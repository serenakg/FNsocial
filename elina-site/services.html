How I help

I lead your project. I bring in who it needs.

My core work is project management and business support. When a project needs more, I connect you with the right people, research your options, or act as your person on the ground in Cyprus.

Core offer

What I do most

01 Core

Project Management

Taking a project from "we should" to done.

I plan it, set the timeline, line up suppliers and contractors, keep the budget honest and handle the paperwork. Most of all, I keep everyone moving.

Good for

  • A project that keeps slipping
  • Work with lots of suppliers or contractors
  • Teams with no one free to run it
02 Core

Business Support

The business work around your project, handled with care.

Following up leads, preparing proposals and tenders, managing relationships, and the admin and small details that matter. So you can get on with what you are good at.

Good for

  • Leads that go cold
  • Proposals and tender submissions
  • Relationships that need looking after

When your project needs more

The right people, checked first

03

Networking and Connecting

Finding and introducing the right people for what you need.

Investors and funders. Co-founders and key hires. Sponsors and collaborators. Accountants, lawyers and other trusted advisers. Every introduction is checked first.

Good for

  • Raising money or finding funders
  • Hiring someone you can trust
  • Finding the right adviser
04

Research

Digging in so you can decide with confidence.

I check people, companies, funders and opportunities. You get clear, plain-English findings: what I found, what it means, and what to watch out for.

Good for

  • Checking a partner before you sign
  • Comparing options side by side
  • Knowing if an opportunity is real
05

Local Liaison

Your person on the ground in Cyprus.

Authorities, permits, suppliers and logistics. If you are entering the market, I help you get set up and running, without the guesswork.

Good for

  • Organisations new to Cyprus
  • Permits and dealing with authorities
  • Finding reliable local suppliers

How it works

Simple, and personal.

  1. We talk

    A short chat. What is stuck, and what good looks like.

  2. I take ownership

    I plan the work and bring in anyone else it needs.

  3. I see it through

    I stay with it, and keep you updated, until it is done.
