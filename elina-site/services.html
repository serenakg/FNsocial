How can I help

Tell me the problem. I will work out who or what can solve it.

Five areas where I do my best work. Most problems touch more than one, and that is fine.

01

Project Management

Taking a project from "we should" to done.

I plan it, set the timeline, line up suppliers and contractors, keep the budget honest and handle the paperwork. Most of all, I keep everyone moving.

Good for

  • A project that keeps slipping
  • Work with lots of suppliers or contractors
  • Teams with no one free to run it
02

Networking and Connecting

Finding and introducing the right people for what you need.

Investors and funders. Co-founders and key hires. Event sponsors and project collaborators. Accountants, lawyers and other trusted advisers.

Every introduction is checked first. You only meet people worth meeting.

Good for

  • Raising money or finding funders
  • Hiring someone you can trust
  • Finding the right adviser
  • Sponsors and collaborators
03

Research

Digging in so you can decide with confidence.

I check people, companies, funders, opportunities and options. You get clear, plain-English findings: what I found, what it means, and what I would watch out for.

Good for

  • Checking a partner before you sign
  • Comparing options side by side
  • Knowing if an opportunity is real
04

Local Liaison

Being your person on the ground in Cyprus.

Authorities, permits, suppliers and logistics. If you are entering the market, I help you get your operation set up and running, without the guesswork.

Good for

  • Organisations new to Cyprus
  • Permits and dealing with authorities
  • Finding reliable local suppliers
05

Business Development and Support

Following up leads, preparing proposals and tenders, and managing relationships.

I also handle the admin and small details that need care. So you can get on with what you are good at.

Good for

  • Leads that go cold
  • Proposals and tender submissions
  • Relationships that need looking after

How it works

Three steps. No mystery.

  1. 01

    Tell me the problem.

    A short chat. What is stuck, what you have tried, what good looks like.

  2. 02

    I find and check.

    I look for the right people or options and check them properly.

  3. 03

    I make the introduction.

    I connect you, explain why, and follow up so it works.

Working together

How we work together depends on what suits you

There is no fixed package. We agree what makes sense once I understand the problem.

  • One-off projectA clear piece of work with a clear finish.
  • Ongoing supportRegular help, month to month.
  • Success-basedWhere it fits, part of my fee depends on the result.
