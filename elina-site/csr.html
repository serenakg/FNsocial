CSR · Corporate social responsibility

CSR that is real, local and measurable.

I help businesses plan and deliver social responsibility work that makes a difference, and I connect them with the NGOs, community groups and causes in Cyprus that can make it happen.

What I do

From good intentions to good work

01

Plan

Work out what your CSR should do, who it is for, and how you will know it worked.

02

Connect

Match companies with NGOs, community groups and causes in Cyprus that fit.

03

Deliver

Planning, coordination, events, volunteers and partners. The practical part, done properly.
