CSR · Corporate social responsibility

CSR that is real, local and measurable.

I help businesses plan and deliver social responsibility work that makes a real difference, and connect them with NGOs, community groups and causes in Cyprus.

Who this is for

For businesses

If you are a business

You want your CSR to mean something, not just fill a report.

  • Find a local cause that fits your values and your team
  • Plan activity with clear, measurable outcomes
  • Coordinate volunteers, events and partners
  • Report back on what actually happened

For NGOs and causes

If you are an NGO or cause

You do the work. You need the right support behind it.

  • Meet businesses that want to help
  • Shape a clear ask they can say yes to
  • Get help planning and running an initiative
  • Build partnerships that last beyond one event
