Femmes, Finances & Freedom

Your body, your money, your policy.

An event on women, money, and the moments that change both.

31 October 2026 · Paphos, Cyprus · 100 attendees (employers, employees & individuals)
Hosted by Serena Gasparini, founder of FemNEST

Get Tickets Become a Sponsor Apply to Speak

Why

We were told: study hard, and it left us in debt. Work hard, and we hit the pay gap. Start a family, and we absorbed the maternity leave penalty and the invisible caregiving load nobody accounts for. Power through, and we burned out. Underneath all four, quietly, our wealth slipped away.

Femmes, Finances & Freedom exists to give women the tools to close those gaps, not just name them. It's a day where the invisible economics of being a woman (at work, in business, alone) get made visible and actionable, for the people living it and the people employing it.

The Purpose

A day where the invisible economics of being a woman (at work, in business, alone) get made visible and actionable, for the people living it and the people employing it.

“We were able to read between the lines, advocate for ourselves, and understand our worth. There is no hiding, we tell real stories. This is our money, our life, and our health, we are self-made.”

Our bar for every session: it has to connect a real health or life transition to something practical you walk away with, a policy, a plan, a number, a next step. Not just naming the problem.

The format

What the day looks like

Main Stage

Open to everyone, all day. Panels and firesides on pay transparency, building a business honestly, and the psychology of working.

Clinics

Deep dive, lane-specific sessions (employer / employee / individual) covering pay advocacy, menopause & the workplace, caregiving, wealth building, and pension rights. Chosen at registration, not gated by ticket price.

Exhibition

An interactive floor, not banners and brochures. Live pay gap gut checks, self-assessment kiosks, and bookable speed mentoring.

Who's in the room

Three audiences, one event

Employers building better workplaces, employees navigating pay and career, and individuals (self-employed or not) building something of their own. Centred on women's peak working years, with rights, policy, and advocacy running through every session.
