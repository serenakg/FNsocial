Femmes, Finances & Freedom

An evening on women, money, and the moments that change both.

About this event

FemNEST's second flagship gathering — building on the sold-out February launch. Femmes, Finances & Freedom brings women together in person to talk honestly about money at the life stages that shape it most: fertility, caregiving, career breaks, menopause, loss, and beyond.

What to expect

The same spirit as the sold-out February launch: real stories from women in the room, not lectures at them. Expect conversation, community, and a room full of women already part of FemNEST's first 50+ — plus space to join them.
