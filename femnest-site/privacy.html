Privacy Policy

Last updated: .

We will always keep your data safe and secure. This policy explains what information we collect about you and how we use it, whether you are a FemNEST community Member, an Attendee at one of our events, a newsletter subscriber, or simply a visitor to our website.

FemNEST is a community-first financial wellness platform for women. This privacy policy is owned and operated by M. S. Project Zeus CY LTD t/a FemNEST.

This policy complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) ("GDPR") and applicable Cyprus data protection law. We process your data securely and transparently.

For the personal data covered by this policy, FemNEST acts as the Data Controller — we determine the purpose and means of processing data collected directly from our Members, Attendees, subscribers and website visitors.

  • M. S. Project Zeus CY LTD t/a FemNEST ("FemNEST")
  • Registered address: Leoforos Tafon Ton Vasileon 118, Pafos, Cyprus 8015
  • Email: hello@yourfemnest.com
  • Website: www.yourfemnest.com
  • Company number: 430641

2. Data protection principles

In relation to personal data, we will:

  • process it fairly, lawfully and in a clear, transparent way;
  • collect personal data only for specific and legitimate reasons;
  • only use it in the way that we have told you about;
  • ensure it is correct and up to date;
  • keep your data for only as long as we require it;
  • process it in a way designed to ensure it is not lost, destroyed or used for anything you are not aware of.

3. Types of data we collect

  • Your name and contact details (email, phone, address) when you book an event ticket, join the Community, subscribe to our newsletter, or contact us;
  • Booking and attendance details for our Events;
  • Payment-related information — this is processed by our third-party ticketing provider (currently Eventbrite or an equivalent platform); FemNEST does not directly receive or store your card details;
  • Community profile information you choose to share, once our Community platform is launched;
  • Photographs and video taken at our Events;
  • Newsletter sign-up details (first name and email address);
  • Website usage data collected via cookies (see our Cookies Policy below).

4. How we collect your data

We collect data about you when you:

  • book a ticket to a FemNEST Event via a third-party ticketing platform;
  • complete a contact, sign-up or membership form on our website;
  • interact with us on social media (including Instagram);
  • attend an Event, including through photography or video taken there;
  • subscribe to our newsletter;
  • in future, join and use our Community platform once launched.

Our ticketing partner shares your relevant booking details with us so that we can manage the Event and communicate with Attendees. Our website is overseen and managed by our web host/provider.

5. Marketing correspondence

If you subscribe to our newsletter via our website or social media, we will send you newsletter updates by email to share news about FemNEST. This is the only marketing correspondence we send. To subscribe, we only ask for your first name and email address.

You can stop receiving marketing emails from us at any time by clicking unsubscribe on our mailing platform, or by emailing hello@yourfemnest.com and telling us you wish to opt out.

6. Photography and media

As set out in our Terms and Conditions, we regularly take photographs and video at our Events for use on our website, social media (including Instagram) and in our marketing. Attending an Event means you consent to this. If you would prefer not to be photographed, please let a member of the FemNEST team know on arrival, and contact us at hello@yourfemnest.com if you would like a specific image removed after the fact.

7. Why we collect your data

  • to sell and manage tickets and admission to our Events;
  • to operate the Community once it launches;
  • to send newsletter updates, where you have subscribed;
  • to improve our services and grow FemNEST, which is in our legitimate interests; you can opt out of marketing at any time;
  • to prevent and detect fraud against you or us.

8. Why we process your data

FemNEST can only process your data for specific purposes permitted by GDPR and Cyprus data protection law:

  • to perform a contract with you (for example, an Event ticket or a Community membership);
  • to pursue our legitimate interests, such as running and improving our Events and Community, where these do not override your rights;
  • on the basis of your consent, for example for photography or newsletter marketing;
  • to comply with a legal obligation, where relevant.

9. If you do not provide your data to us

If you do not provide the data we need, we may not be able to process your Event booking, Community membership, or respond to your enquiry.

10. Sharing your data

We will never sell your personal data to any third party. We share data only where necessary to provide our services, and do not share it with anyone outside the European Economic Area, except as set out below.

We may share your data with:

  • our ticketing platform provider (currently Eventbrite or an equivalent);
  • our website host/provider;
  • our future Community platform provider, once selected;
  • professional advisors, where necessary to run FemNEST;
  • the police, or a regulatory or government authority, if legally required to investigate illegal activity.

11. Protecting your data

We take appropriate technical and organisational steps to protect your data against accidental loss, disclosure, destruction or misuse, including limiting access to those who need it, using appropriately secure systems, and requiring written agreements with any third party that processes data on our behalf.

The internet is not a completely secure method of communication, so we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information sent to us online, though we take great care to protect it.

12. How long we keep your data

We keep your data only for as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected. You can ask us to erase your data at any time, in writing where possible, and we conduct periodic reviews to delete data we no longer need.

13. Automated decision making

No decision with a significant impact on you will ever be made about you solely on the basis of automated decision-making, without human involvement.

14. Your rights in respect of your data

GDPR and Cyprus data protection law give you the following rights, in summary:

  • The right to be informed how we use your data (the purpose of this policy).
  • The right of access to the data we hold about you — make a request to Serena Kara Gasparini at hello@yourfemnest.com.
  • The right to have inaccurate data corrected.
  • The right to have your data deleted, where there is no reason for us to continue processing it.
  • The right to restrict processing while accuracy or another issue is resolved.
  • The right to data portability.
  • The right to object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time, without consequence, for anything we process on the basis of your consent.

15. Amendments to our privacy policy

We may amend this policy from time to time, while ensuring it continues to comply with GDPR and Cyprus law. We will post the most up-to-date version on our website and may announce material changes on our social media.

16. If you wish to make a complaint

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please contact Serena Kara Gasparini at hello@yourfemnest.com so we can investigate. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection.

17. Data Protection contact

Our data protection contact is Serena Kara Gasparini, who can be reached at hello@yourfemnest.com.

Cookies Policy

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a piece of data sent from a website and stored on your device. When you visit our website again, the data stored in the cookie can be retrieved to recognise your previous activity.

You must have cookies enabled to book tickets, join the Community, or contact us through our website.

Our cookies do not store personal information such as your name, contact number, email or postal address, but may allow us to access this information once you are logged in or contacting us via our website.

www.yourfemnest.com uses cookies to:

  • identify you when you visit our website or contact us through it;
  • remember your preferences and past activity on our site;
  • allow you to share our content on your own social media platforms;
  • improve our website, content and services;
  • offer you a better experience with FemNEST.

How can you manage your cookies?

To review, limit, block or delete cookies from our website, use your browser settings to manage cookie preferences.
