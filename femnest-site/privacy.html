Privacy Policy

Last updated: . This is a plain-language starting point — see the note below before treating it as final.

This is a template drafted to cover the site's current, actual data practices — it is not legal advice. Have a lawyer review it against Cyprus/EU law (GDPR) before relying on it.

Who we are

FemNEST ("we", "us", "our") is a pre-seed company based in Cyprus, building a platform connecting women's health and life transitions to their financial outcomes. This policy explains what personal data this website collects, why, and what rights you have over it.

What we collect

Right now, this site collects personal data in two ways:

  • Waitlist sign-up: if you join the waitlist, we collect your name and email address via our email provider, Kit (ConvertKit), so we can send you updates about FemNEST.
  • Direct contact: if you email us or fill in an inquiry link, we collect whatever you send us (e.g. name, email, message) to respond to you.

We do not currently run analytics or advertising cookies on this site. If that changes, this section will be updated first, and a cookie banner will ask for your consent before anything loads — see "Cookies" below.

How we use it

We use your data to: send waitlist and community updates; respond to inquiries; and, if you're an event guest, to manage your registration. We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with third parties except the service providers who help us run the site (currently Kit, for email).

Cookies

This site uses essential cookies/local storage to remember your cookie preference itself. Analytics cookies (Google Analytics, Hotjar) are built into the site but not currently active — they're switched off pending real configuration and your consent. When they do go live, the cookie banner will ask you to accept or decline before any analytics script loads.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict our use of it, and to data portability. To exercise any of these, email serena@yourfemnest.com.

Data retention

We keep waitlist and contact data for as long as you remain subscribed or until you ask us to delete it. You can unsubscribe from emails at any time via the link in any email we send.

Third-party services

We currently use Kit (ConvertKit) to manage the waitlist and send emails. Their own privacy policy governs how they process data on our behalf.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: serena@yourfemnest.com.

FemNEST is currently in pilot stage — MVP launching Spring 2027.
