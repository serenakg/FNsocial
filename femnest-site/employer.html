Bring FemNEST to your workplace

A structured pilot to support your people through life's biggest financial transitions — and get ahead of the EU Pay Transparency Directive.

Why now

The Directive changes the calculus

Large employers must report gender pay gap data under the EU Pay Transparency Directive by June 2027. A report alone doesn't close a gap — it just measures it. FemNEST's pilot gives your organisation real support for the transitions driving that gap: fertility, caregiving, menopause, career breaks, and more.

What's included

Built around your people, not a generic benefit

Education

Sessions and resources on the financial impact of the life transitions your workforce is actually navigating.

Community

Employee access to FemNEST's community and peer support alongside women navigating the same life stages.

Reporting support

Structured input to inform your pay-gap reporting and wellbeing initiatives ahead of the June 2027 deadline.
