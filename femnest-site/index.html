The framework

Mind the Gap

How did this happen? Her body and life changed. Her income followed. Nobody connected the two.

This is the story FemNEST was built to change, for one woman first. Meet the founder who's living it.

Adolescence & puberty → Early career 54% Education Gap

More than half of young women enter the workforce already behind on financial literacy and confidence.

Early career → Fertility & health €0.85 Wage Gap

For every €1 a man earns, a woman doing the same work takes home €0.85, a gap that starts on day one.

Fertility & health → Maternity & pregnancy 1 in 4 Health Gap

1 in 4 women face a fertility or health challenge during this stage that comes with a real financial cost.

Maternity & pregnancy → Caregiving 16 Years Pay Gap

The pay and progression lost around motherhood adds up to the equivalent of 16 years of income over a lifetime.

Caregiving → Perimenopause & menopause 7.7M Caregiving Gap

7.7 million women across Europe step back from paid work for caregiving, and most never fully catch back up financially.

Perimenopause & menopause → Retirement 22% Pension & Wealth Gap

Every gap before this one compounds: women retire with pensions 22% smaller than men’s.

These gaps don't happen in isolation: they cascade across a lifetime. No tool exists to protect a woman through them.

Sources: EIGE 2025 · Eurostat 2024 · WEF Global Gender Gap Report 2024.

Why FemNEST exists

I founded FemNEST because I lived the gap between needing support and finding it.

I'm Serena Gasparini, and my journey to this stage wasn't paved with corporate ladders, but with life-altering pivots. After years in the corporate world, I hit a wall of burnout that forced me to reclaim my time through freelancing. But life had more in store: I was simultaneously navigating the silent battle of a lifelong PMOS diagnosis and the physical toll of fertility treatments.

When I finally held my son, the joy was met with a new challenge: he was born with a medical condition that required my full presence. In those moments, carrying the emotional weight of a “no backup plan” reality, I realised how quickly a woman's independence can feel like it's slipping away. I asked myself every time: why is financial stability so hard to come by for women going through life's biggest changes?

Through my marketing agency, Sense and Forum, I began hearing my own story reflected in hundreds of others. Whether it's health, motherhood, or career shifts, we're often one transition away from losing our footing. Based in Cyprus, where women still face systemic barriers like requiring a spouse's signature for basic banking, I built the platform I needed and couldn't find.

The idea

What is FemNEST

FemNEST is the first platform mapping women's health and life transitions (fertility, caregiving, career breaks, menopause, loss, and more) to their real financial outcomes. It's community-first, built from lived experience, and designed to give women the knowledge, tools, and people around them to make informed financial decisions at every life stage.

Community

Peer & professional support network: a community of women living the same transitions, supported by verified professionals, online and IRL.

Education

Financial literacy tools & events: EU Pay Transparency Directive training so everyone learns their legal rights, their financial reality, and how to close the gap.

Investment Tools

Life-health-to-finance tracking, connecting 50+ female health and life transitions to personalised financial plans. No competitor has built this: it's our owned IP.

Advocacy

Data insights for employers & policymakers: aggregated, anonymised data revealing the care-finance connection, powering smarter workplace and policy interventions.

AI agent, coming soon

Your personal FemNEST assistant: it'll connect the dots between your own life transitions and your finances automatically, and flag the moments (like these four pillars) when it's time to act.
